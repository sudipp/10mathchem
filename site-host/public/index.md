# Self Learning: pick a subject, start small

Free, self-paced lessons for 10th-grade chemistry, math and world history by Perspire AI. Short lessons, step-by-step animations, videos, and homework help you reveal one step at a time. No account needed.

## Chemistry: atoms, electrons & bonds

Now available: Chapter 1, Measurement & Reference Sheets; Chapter 2, Atomic Theory & Nuclear Chemistry; Chapter 3, Electron Configuration & Periodic Trends; and Chapter 4, Lewis Structures & Nomenclature. Animated lessons, narrated videos with exam reviews, a periodic table, and step-by-step help for every worksheet and unit review.

[Open chemistry](https://perspireai.com/chemistry/)

## Math, one unit at a time

Now available: Unit 0, Linear, Absolute Value & Piecewise Functions; Unit 1, Quadratic Functions; Unit 2, Solving Quadratics, Systems & Inequalities; Unit 3, Conic Sections; Unit 4, Polynomial Functions; and Unit 5, Radical Expressions, with the Fall Final Exam Review. Lessons, videos, and step-by-step homework help for every assignment. More units are on the way.

[Open math](https://perspireai.com/math/)

## World history, one chapter a day

Now available: Chapters 10–24, from the Greek city-states and Rome through the Silk Roads, Islam, East Asia, India and the Indian Ocean, Christendom, the Mongols, Africa, medieval Europe and the Americas, to the transoceanic voyages and early modern Europe (to 1800). Each chapter is an hour-long plan: the big question, an interactive timeline, an animated map, a short lesson with cause and effect, flashcards, a narrated video, and AP-style practice with sources, short answers and an essay outline.
