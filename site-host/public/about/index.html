About Self Learning

Self Learning is a free, self-paced study site for 10th-grade chemistry and math, made by Perspire AI. It started as a way to turn real class notes and worksheets into lessons a student can work through alone, at their own speed.

What you'll find

  • Chemistry: Chapter 4, Lewis Structures & Nomenclature, with an interactive periodic table and worksheet help.
  • Math: organized by unit. Unit 4, Polynomial Functions, has fourteen narrated video lessons and homework help for HW #1–#15.

How it works

Each lesson is a short reading with step-by-step animations and a video. Homework help shows one step at a time, so you can check your thinking before you see the full answer. There are no accounts, ads or sign-ups.

Videos

Some lessons embed videos by Khan Academy and Tyler DeWitt from YouTube. Those videos remain with their creators; Self Learning is not affiliated with them.
