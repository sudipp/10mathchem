About Self Learning

Self Learning is a free, self-paced study site for 10th-grade chemistry and math, made by Perspire AI. It started as a way to turn real class notes and worksheets into lessons a student can work through alone, at their own speed.

What you'll find

  • Chemistry: Chapters 1–4 (measurement, atomic theory and nuclear chemistry, electron configuration and periodic trends, Lewis structures and nomenclature), with an interactive periodic table and worksheet help.
  • Math: organized by unit. Unit 0, Linear, Absolute Value & Piecewise Functions, has five narrated video lessons and homework help for HW #1–#6; Unit 1, Quadratic Functions, has five narrated video lessons and homework help for HW #1–#7; Unit 2, Solving Quadratics, Systems & Inequalities, has six narrated video lessons and homework help for HW #11–#19; Unit 3, Conic Sections, has eight narrated video lessons and homework help for HW #1–#8; Unit 4, Polynomial Functions, has fourteen narrated video lessons and homework help for HW #1–#17; Unit 5, Radical Expressions, has three narrated video lessons and homework help for HW #1 and the Fall Final Exam Review.

How it works

Each lesson is a short reading with step-by-step animations and a video. Homework help shows one step at a time, so you can check your thinking before you see the full answer. There are no accounts, ads or sign-ups.

Videos

Some lessons embed videos by Khan Academy and Tyler DeWitt from YouTube. Those videos remain with their creators; Self Learning is not affiliated with them.
